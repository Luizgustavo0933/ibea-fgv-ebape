07 / Questions & answers

Good questions. Clear answers.

An introduction for future students, families and high school counselors.

Can high school students apply directly to IBEA?

No. At FGV EBAPE, students must first enroll in the undergraduate Business Administration degree and later apply for IBEA through the school's selection process.

How many partner institutions participate?

Four: FGV EBAPE (Brazil), University of Mannheim Business School (Germany), Darla Moore School of Business at the University of South Carolina (USA), and ESSEC Business School's Singapore campus.

Where do FGV EBAPE students study during the program?

After their first two years at FGV EBAPE, selected students study at partner schools in Germany, the United States and Singapore before returning to Brazil to complete the degree.

Does IBEA charge additional host-school tuition?

According to FGV EBAPE, students continue paying tuition to their home institution and do not pay additional tuition to host schools. Travel, accommodation, insurance, visa costs and personal expenses remain the student's responsibility.

How can a high school request more information?

Use the contact button on this page to email FGV EBAPE, or consult the official program page for updated admission requirements and selection details.
